Our Story

From friendship to forever

Some love stories begin with a first date. Ours began much earlier.

A story written long before we knew it.

James and Claudia's story began during their childhood days as childhood friends.

Life eventually took them on different journeys, travelling apart on their individual paths of self-discovery, growth and becoming the people they were meant to be.

Yet sometimes, the paths that appear to separate us are simply preparing us for the moment when they cross again.

Years later, they reconnected.

Two childhood friends. Two first-born children. Two families who already knew each other.

And somehow, the friendship that began in childhood found its way into something deeper.

From childhood friends, to reunited hearts, to forever.
